About Vitania Care

Healthier People, Brighter Workplaces

Vitania Care Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based healthcare organisation committed to delivering comprehensive, accessible and personalised healthcare solutions across India. We partner with corporates, institutions and families to promote better health outcomes through preventive healthcare, Employee Wellness Programs and comprehensive home healthcare services. Our focus is on making quality healthcare more accessible, convenient and patient-centric at every stage of life.

Our Story

Making Quality Healthcare Easy to Access

Most people visit a doctor only when something goes wrong. We founded Vitania Care to change this by bringing regular Health Check-ups, expert medical advice and timely care to the places where people spend most of their time: at work and at home.

Today, we partner with organisations to look after their employees and with families to care for their loved ones. Every program is led by qualified professionals and coordinated by a dedicated team.

Our Vision

To ensure that every employee and every family has easy access to preventive, coordinated healthcare.

Our Mission

What We Work Towards Every Day

  • Help people identify health risks early and take timely action.
  • Make doctors, counsellors and nurses easily accessible.
  • Coordinate care so that nothing is overlooked.
  • Provide employers with clear, confidential insights into workforce health.
