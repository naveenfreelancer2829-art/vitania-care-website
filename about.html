About Vitania Care

Healthier people, brighter workplaces

Vitania Care Private Limited is a Bengaluru-based healthcare company. We help organisations and families stay healthy through preventive care, wellness programmes and medical care at home.

Contact usCall +91 97026 50245
Our story

Making good healthcare simple to access

Most people visit a doctor only when something goes wrong. We started Vitania Care to change that, by bringing regular check-ups, expert advice and timely care to the places where people spend their time: at work and at home.

Today, we partner with employers to look after their teams and with families to look after their loved ones. Every programme is led by qualified professionals and coordinated by one dedicated team.

Our vision

A future where every employee and every family has easy access to preventive, coordinated healthcare.

Our mission

What we work towards every day

  • Help people detect health risks early and act on them
  • Make doctors, counsellors and nurses easy to reach
  • Coordinate care so that nothing falls through the gaps
  • Give employers clear, confidential insight into workforce health
