FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a corporate wellness programme?

A typical programme combines health check-ups, doctor consultations, health talks, fitness and nutrition sessions, and support for chronic conditions. We tailor the mix to your workforce.

Can sessions be conducted online?

Yes. Webinars, tele-consultations, counselling and nutrition coaching can all be delivered online for remote and hybrid teams.

How do you measure the impact of the programme?

We track participation, screening results and follow-up outcomes, and share anonymised quarterly reports with your HR team.

How long does it take to launch?

Launch timelines depend on the services you choose. Simple programmes can usually begin within a few weeks of signing.
