Wellness Programs That Keep Your Workforce Healthy

Program Inclusions

Everything Your Team Needs to Stay Well

Choose individual services or combine them into one annual program.

Health Risk Assessments

Short screenings that identify lifestyle and health risks across your workforce.

On-site Health Camps

Camps at your workplace for blood pressure, blood sugar, BMI, eye, dental and other checks.

Health Talks & Webinars

Expert-led sessions by doctors on topics such as heart health, diabetes, sleep and posture.

Fitness & Yoga Sessions

Group yoga, stretching and fitness sessions conducted at your office or online.

Nutrition Coaching

Personalised diet plans and one-to-one guidance from qualified nutritionists.

Chronic Care Programs

Ongoing support for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders and weight management.

Fitness & Recovery

Building Healthy Habits That Last

Long hours at a desk can lead to back pain, stiffness and fatigue. Our physiotherapists and fitness coaches help employees move better and feel better.

  • Ergonomic Assessments: Workstation reviews and posture correction.
  • Physiotherapy Support: Treatment for back, neck and joint pain.
  • Fitness Challenges: Monthly activities that keep teams active and engaged.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an Employee Wellness Program include?

A typical program combines Health Check-ups, Doctor Consultations, health talks, fitness and nutrition sessions, and support for chronic conditions. We tailor the combination to your workforce.

Can sessions be conducted online?

Yes. Webinars, tele-consultations, counselling and nutrition coaching can all be delivered online for remote and hybrid teams across India.

How do you measure the impact of the program?

We track participation, screening results and follow-up outcomes, and share anonymised quarterly reports with your HR team.

How long does it take to launch a program?

Launch timelines depend on the services selected. Most standard programs can begin within a few weeks of confirmation.
