What we offer

Everything your team needs to stay well

Choose individual services or combine them into a year-round wellness programme.

Doctor tele-consultations

Audio and video consultations with general physicians and specialists.

On-site health centres

Doctors and nurses placed at your office to run an occupational health centre.

Medical camps

Health camps at your workplace with general physicians and specialists.

Radiology tests

X-ray, ultrasound and other imaging arranged through partner centres.

Annual health check-ups

Yearly check-up packages, with reports explained by a doctor.

Pre-employment check-ups

Medical fitness checks for new joiners, done quickly and on schedule.

Mental health support

Confidential sessions with counsellors and psychiatrists.

Chronic disease programmes

Prevention and management for diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid and more.

Health talks and webinars

Sessions led by doctors and specialists on topics your team cares about.

Preventive health programmes

Screening and vaccination drives planned across the year.

Lifestyle and nutrition coaching

Guidance on diet, activity, sleep and stress from qualified coaches.

Care for employees' families

Home care and elder care extended to your employees' parents and families.

How we work with you

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can the programme be customised for our company?

Yes. We design every programme around your team size, office locations, health priorities and budget.

Do you conduct camps at our office?

Yes. We bring doctors, nurses and equipment to your workplace and plan camps around your working hours.

Is employee health information kept confidential?

Yes. Individual results are shared only with the employee. Companies receive summary-level reports without personal details.

Can you cover our employees' families?

Yes. Home care and elder care services can be extended to employees' parents and families.

How do we start?

Send us an enquiry. We will meet your HR team, understand your needs and share a proposal.
