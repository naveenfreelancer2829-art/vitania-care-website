Occupational Health Centre (OHC)

Comprehensive Medical Facilities at Your Workplace

We provide end-to-end setup and management of Occupational Health Centres at your premises, ensuring employees have convenient access to timely medical care, preventive health services, and emergency support without leaving the workplace.

  • Qualified Medical Professionals: Deployment of experienced MBBS doctors and trained nursing staff, with schedules aligned to your operational hours and shift requirements.
  • First Aid & Emergency Response: Immediate medical assistance, first aid management, and coordination with nearby hospitals during medical emergencies.
  • Medical Examinations & Health Records: Pre-employment and periodic medical assessments, along with secure and confidential employee health record management.
Teleconsultation Services

Convenient Access to General Physicians & Specialist Doctors

We provide seamless access to qualified general physicians and specialist doctors through phone and video consultations, enabling employees to receive timely medical advice without disrupting their work schedules or visiting hospitals.

  • General Physician ConsultationsMedical consultations for common illnesses, infections, lifestyle-related conditions, and routine health concerns.
  • Specialist ConsultationsAccess to experienced specialists for further evaluation, expert medical opinions, and condition-specific guidance.
  • Digital Prescriptions & Follow-upsOnline prescriptions, medicine delivery support, and follow-up consultations to ensure continuity of care.
Fitness & Recovery

Promoting Movement, Fitness & Workplace Wellbeing

We offer professionally guided fitness and recovery programmes to reduce work-related physical discomfort, improve mobility, and encourage healthier workplace habits.

  • Ergonomic Assessments: Workstation evaluations and posture improvement guidance.
  • Physiotherapy Support: Professional care for back, neck, and joint-related concerns.
  • Fitness Initiatives: Engaging fitness challenges and wellness activities to promote an active workforce.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a typical corporate wellness programme include?

Our programmes may include Occupational Health Centres, Onsite Health Check-ups, Teleconsultations, health webinars, fitness and nutrition sessions, and chronic disease management. Each programme is customised to your workforce's healthcare requirements.

Can the programme support remote and hybrid employees?

Yes. Teleconsultations, webinars, counselling, and nutrition coaching can be delivered virtually, ensuring healthcare access for remote and hybrid employees across India.

How do you measure programme effectiveness?

We monitor employee participation, health screening outcomes, and follow-up activities, providing quarterly anonymised reports to help HR teams assess programme performance.

How soon can we launch the programme?

Implementation timelines depend on the services selected and operational requirements. Most standard programmes can be initiated within a few weeks of confirmation.
