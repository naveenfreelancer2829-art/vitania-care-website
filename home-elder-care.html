FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How soon can a doctor or nurse visit?

Visits can often be arranged within a day. Please tell us how urgent your need is when you call.

Is home care suitable for emergencies?

No. Home care is not a replacement for emergency services. In a medical emergency, call an ambulance or go to the nearest hospital.

What does an elder care manager do?

A care manager, who is a trained nurse, coordinates doctor visits, medicines, tests and home support, and keeps the family updated.

Can companies offer this as an employee benefit?

Yes. Companies can extend home and elder care to employees' families as part of their wellness programme.
