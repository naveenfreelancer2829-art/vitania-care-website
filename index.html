FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does Vitania Care offer?

We provide corporate wellness programmes, health check-ups, doctor consultations, mental wellbeing support, and home and elder care. Companies can choose individual services or a complete year-round programme.

Can the programme be customised for our company?

Yes. Every programme is planned around your team size, office locations, work shifts, health priorities and budget.

Do you work with small companies and start-ups?

Yes. We work with teams of all sizes, from early-stage start-ups to large organisations with several offices.

Is employee health data kept confidential?

Yes. Individual health records are shared only with the employee. Employers receive anonymised, summary-level reports.

Which locations do you cover?

We are based in Bengaluru and serve offices and homes across the city. For other locations, please contact us to discuss your requirements.

Ready to invest in your team's health?

Tell us about your organisation and we will share a wellness plan and proposal tailored to your needs.
