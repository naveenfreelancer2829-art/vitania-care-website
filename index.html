FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does Vitania Care do?

Vitania Care provides preventive and ongoing healthcare in three ways: wellness programmes for companies, medical care at home, and coordinated care for elderly parents.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Bengaluru and serve offices and homes across the city. For company programmes in other cities, contact us and we will confirm what we can offer.

How is this different from visiting a hospital or clinic?

Care comes to you, and the same team stays with you over time. You get a care plan and a single point of contact instead of one-off appointments.

Do I need any special device or app?

No. Most of our services work over a phone call, WhatsApp or video call. Doctors and nurses visit in person where needed.

How do I get started?

Fill in the enquiry form or contact us. Our team will call you back to understand your needs and suggest the right plan.
