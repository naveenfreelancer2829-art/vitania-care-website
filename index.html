FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our corporate healthcare solutions, service delivery, employee wellness programs and home healthcare services.

What does Vitania Care offer?

For organisations, we offer Occupational Health Centres (OHC), Onsite Health Check-ups, Onsite Camps, Teleconsultation, Lab Tests, Mental Wellbeing support and Employee Wellness Programs. For individuals and families, we provide Home Care and Caregiver Services. Organisations can choose individual services or a comprehensive year-round program.

Can individuals and families use your services?

Yes. Individuals and families can book Home Care, Caregiver Services, physiotherapy, lab tests at home, and medicines and medical equipment directly with us.

Can the program be customised for our organisation?

Yes. Every program is planned around your team size, office locations, work shifts, health priorities and budget.

Do you work with small companies and start-ups?

Yes. We work with organisations of all sizes, from early-stage start-ups to large enterprises with multiple offices.

Is employee health data kept confidential?

Yes. Individual health records are shared only with the employee concerned. Employers receive anonymised, summary-level reports.

Which locations do you cover?

We cover locations across India. Onsite services, such as Occupational Health Centres, Onsite Health Check-ups and Onsite Camps, are arranged at your office locations, while Teleconsultation, counselling, webinars and seminars are available nationwide.
