FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Vitania Care offer?

We offer Employee Wellness Programs, Health Check-ups, Doctor Consultations, Mental Wellbeing support and Home Care services. Organisations can choose individual services or a comprehensive year-round program.

Can the program be customised for our organisation?

Yes. Every program is planned around your team size, office locations, work shifts, health priorities and budget.

Do you work with small companies and start-ups?

Yes. We work with organisations of all sizes, from early-stage start-ups to large enterprises with multiple offices.

Is employee health data kept confidential?

Yes. Individual health records are shared only with the employee concerned. Employers receive anonymised, summary-level reports.

Which locations do you serve?

We serve organisations across India. Tele-consultations, counselling and webinars are available nationwide, and on-site services are arranged according to your office locations.

Ready to Build a Healthier Workforce?

Tell us about your organisation, and we will share a wellness plan and proposal tailored to your requirements.
