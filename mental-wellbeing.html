Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Confidential Counselling for a Healthier, More Resilient Workforce

We provide confidential mental health and emotional wellbeing support through qualified counsellors, helping employees navigate workplace stress, anxiety, personal challenges, and emotional concerns. Employees can access professional counselling through flexible phone, video, or in-person sessions, ensuring convenient, compassionate, and personalised support.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are counselling sessions confidential?

Yes. All sessions are strictly confidential. Employee participation and personal discussions are not disclosed to employers.

Who can access the service?

All employees covered under the programme are eligible. Family members can also be included based on the selected plan.

How can employees book a session?

Employees can contact our support team to schedule phone, video, or in-person counselling at their convenience.

What concerns can counsellors address?

Counselling support covers stress, anxiety, emotional wellbeing, relationship challenges, parenting, grief, workplace pressure, and work–life balance.
