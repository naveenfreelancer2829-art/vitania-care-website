FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. Sessions are completely confidential. Employers never see who used the service or what was discussed.

Who can use the Employee Assistance Programme?

All employees covered by your plan can use it, and family members can be included if you choose.

How do employees book a session?

Employees can call or message our team to book a phone, video or in-person session at a convenient time.

What topics can counsellors help with?

Stress, anxiety, low mood, relationships, parenting, grief, work pressure and work–life balance, among others.
