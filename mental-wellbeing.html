Why It Matters

A Supported Team Is a Productive Team

Unaddressed stress affects focus, attendance and retention. Early, confidential support helps employees stay well and perform at their best.

  • Flexible Sessions: Available by phone, video or in person.
  • Easy Booking: No paperwork required for employees.
  • Complete Privacy: Every conversation remains confidential.
FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. All sessions are completely confidential. Employers are never informed about who used the service or what was discussed.

Who can use the Employee Assistance Program?

All employees covered under your plan can use the service, and family members can be included if you choose.

How do employees book a session?

Employees can call or message our team to book a phone, video or in-person session at a convenient time.

What concerns can counsellors help with?

Counsellors can help with stress, anxiety, low mood, relationships, parenting, grief, work pressure and work–life balance, among other concerns.
